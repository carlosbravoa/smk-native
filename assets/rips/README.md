# Ripped sprite sheets (LOCAL ONLY - never committed)

The hand-supplied sheets the art generators read: `tools/labs/itemsheet.py`,
`molesheet.py`, `fishsheet.py`, `rrthwomp.py`, `flatsheet.py` turn these into
the `src/*art.inc` tables.

These are GAME ARTWORK and are deliberately **not** tracked - see the note at
the top of `.gitignore`.  They lived in `tmp/` until a clean-up nearly took
them with it; nothing that is needed belongs in a scratch directory.

They cannot be regenerated by this repository: supply your own.
